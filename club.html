Club FAQ

Anything else? Send us a note. Already a member? Manage your membership.

When will I be charged?

On the day you join, then on the same date each billing period (monthly, every 3 months, or yearly). You'll get an email receipt every time.

When does my first sheet ship?

Sheets ship on the 1st. Join by 11:59pm PT on the 25th to make that month's mailing; after that, you'll start with the next month's sheet.

Can I skip, pause or cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your membership portal. Cancel before your renewal date and you won't be charged again. You keep any sheet you've already paid for.

Do you ship internationally?

We do! Club shipping is free in the US; Canada and international members pay a small shipping fee shown at checkout.

Can I buy past sheets?

Sometimes. If there are leftovers after the club mailing, they go into the shop, and members get first dibs.
