This Privacy Policy explains how [Legal business name] ("The Sticky Kind", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit our website, join the sticky kind club, or buy from our shop.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, email address and, if you choose, a phone number.
  • Shipping details: mailing address and, for gifts, the recipient's name, address and your gift note.
  • Order and membership details: what you bought, your plan, billing dates and order history.
  • Messages: anything you send through our contact form, email or social media.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number is entered directly with Stripe and never touches or is stored on our servers. We receive limited details from Stripe such as card brand, last four digits, expiry date and billing country. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Information collected automatically

Basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages visited and approximate location (from IP address), collected through essential cookies and, only if you agree, analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To process orders, run your club membership, and ship your stickers
  • To send receipts, shipping updates, renewal reminders and account notices
  • To answer your questions and provide support
  • To send marketing emails, only if you opt in; unsubscribe anytime
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure
  • To understand what's working and improve the shop (with consent where required)
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations

Where the GDPR / UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests (running and improving a small business, preventing fraud), your consent (marketing and analytics), and legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the shop:

WhoWhy
StripePayments, subscriptions, customer portal
Shipping carriers [e.g. USPS, Canada Post]Delivering your orders
Email provider [e.g. Mailchimp / Klaviyo]Order and marketing emails
Hosting [e.g. Vercel / Netlify]Running this website
Analytics [e.g. Plausible]Understanding site usage (with consent)

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar storage:

  • Essential: keep your bag and checkout working and remember your cookie choice. These can't be switched off.
  • Analytics (optional): help us understand which pages and stickers people like. Only set if you click "Sounds good".

You can change your choice anytime using "Cookie settings" in the footer, or by clearing your browser storage.

How long we keep it

We keep order and membership records for as long as your account is active and as needed for tax and accounting (generally up to 7 years). Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. Messages are kept for up to 2 years.

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or download your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

California residents: you have the rights described in the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined.

EU/UK residents: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email hello@thestickykind.com. We'll respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity.

Security

We use reputable providers, HTTPS throughout the site and limited access to customer data. No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

Children

Our shop is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. Purchases must be made by an adult.

International transfers

We're based in [country] and our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes & contact

We'll post any changes here and update the date above. For significant changes we'll let members know by email.

Questions? Email hello@thestickykind.com or write to [Legal business name], [Business mailing address].
