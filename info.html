hi, hello

Made by hand, one sheet at a time

The Sticky Kind started with a sketchbook, a crayon-textured brush and a slightly embarrassing sticker collection.

Every design is drawn by hand, then printed in small batches on matte vinyl. Each month gets a theme, from cozy kitchens to holiday mornings, and becomes one sheet for the club.

We pack every order ourselves, write the little notes ourselves and read every message ourselves. Thanks for being here, and for being the sticky kind. ♡

Sticker specs & care

Our stickers are matte white vinyl with a gentle, permanent adhesive. Waterproof, scratch-resistant and fade-resistant indoors.

🧼

Clean firstWipe the surface so it's dry and dust-free before sticking.

👆

Press from the middleSmooth outwards to push out any air bubbles.

💧

⏳

frequently asked

FAQ

Club-specific questions are on the club page too.

How big are the stickers?

Sheets are 5 × 7 in. Stickers on the sheet range from about 0.75 to 2.5 in. Shop singles are 3 to 3.5 in.

Are they waterproof?

Yes. They handle splashes, rain and hand-washing. Avoid the dishwasher and long soaks.

Can I use your designs commercially?

Stickers are for personal use: journals, gifts, your stuff. Please don't scan, reproduce or resell the designs. For collabs or licensing, get in touch.

My order arrived damaged or never showed up

Oh no! Email us within 30 days with your order number (and a photo if it's damaged) and we'll replace it. See our shipping policy.

How do I change my address or payment card?

Use your membership portal. Address changes made after the 25th apply to the following month.

Do you do wholesale?

We're starting to! Shops and stockists can email us for our wholesale sheet.
